How to play

Each day brings a real hand from the World Series of Poker. Every player's cards are face up except one. Guess that player's two hole cards.

You get one guess per street: preflop, flop, draw and river. After each guess the next street is dealt, along with its betting. Use the ◀ ▶ arrows on the table (or your arrow keys) to look back at earlier streets.

A♠ GreenOrange: right card. It locks in.
7♠ YellowBlue outline: right suit, wrong rank.
4♦ RedGray outline: wrong suit.
▲Higher ▼Lower ✓Right rank
The card's color tells you about the suit. The tag beside the card tells you about the rank: whether the real card is higher or lower (aces high), or that you have the right rank.

Each column always tracks the same hidden card, so hints in one column all describe the same card.

Missed a day? Open the archive (the calendar button) to play any hand from the last 30 days. Archive hands don't count toward your streak, but a day you missed is added to your stats.

Hands come from public tournament reporting. A source link appears after you finish.

Bugs or suggestions? Email pokerdledotnet@gmail.com.

Turn on Easy Mode?

Easy Mode shows each player's chance of winning the hand on every street.

Today's hand won't count toward your streak, and your current streak will reset. Your result will be shared as Easy Mode.
